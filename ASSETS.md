# Asset attribution

This project is an **unofficial fan companion** and is not affiliated with,
endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

## What's included

- `public/images/cast/*` — cropped, resized stills from Rockstar's official
  GTA VI screenshot releases (May 2025), used to illustrate confirmed cast
  entries.
- `public/images/locations/*` — cropped, resized stills from the same
  official release, used to illustrate confirmed in-game locations.
- `public/images/hero/*` — a cropped, resized still used as a background
  hero image.

All of the above are copyrighted promotional material owned by **Rockstar
Games / Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc.** They're included here at
reduced resolution, cropped/recompressed from the originals, solely for
commentary, identification, and fan-reference purposes — not sold,
monetized, or presented as original work.

Trailer embeds on the Media and Dispatch pages link directly to Rockstar's
own official YouTube channel; no video files are hosted in this repository.

## Removal requests

If you are a rights holder and want any asset removed, open an issue or
contact the maintainer directly — it will be removed promptly, no
questions asked.
